[MetricKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metrickit) is Apple's framework for the diagnostics and metrics the operating system collects about your app on your users' devices. The MetricKit integration converts two kinds of MetricKit data:

- **Diagnostic reports**, such as app hangs, CPU and disk write exceptions, or slow app launches, become Sentry error events. Learn more in <PlatformLink to="/metric-kit/diagnostic-reports/">App Hangs and Other Diagnostic Reports</PlatformLink>.
- **Metric reports**, such as CPU time, network transfer, memory and app exit counts, become <PlatformLink to="/metrics/">Sentry metrics</PlatformLink>. Learn more in <PlatformLink to="/metric-kit/metric-reports/">Metric Reports</PlatformLink>.

The integration is disabled by default, and you enable diagnostic reports and metric reports separately.

## Prerequisites

- SDK version 8.14.0 or later for diagnostic reports, and 9.32.0 or later for metric reports.
- iOS 15 and later, macOS 12 and later, or visionOS. MetricKit isn't available on tvOS and watchOS.
- MetricKit only collects data from users who agreed to share analytics with app developers.

## Delayed Data

MetricKit reports data after it was recorded. It delivers diagnostic reports on the next launch of the app, and metric payloads about once a day. The SDK sends this data with the app and OS versions it was recorded with, instead of the versions running when MetricKit delivered it.
