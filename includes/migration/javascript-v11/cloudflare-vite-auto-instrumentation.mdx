`sentryCloudflareVitePlugin()` wraps your Worker entry at build time, along with any Durable Object, Workflow, or WorkerEntrypoint class listed in your wrangler config. Entries you wrapped yourself are left untouched, so most setups need no changes. The experimental `_experimental.autoInstrumentation` opt-in was removed, and you can opt out with the top-level `autoInstrumentation` option:

```js {filename:vite.config.mjs}
sentryCloudflareVitePlugin({ autoInstrumentation: false });
```
