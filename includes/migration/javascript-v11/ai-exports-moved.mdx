AI instrumentation moved from `@sentry/core` to `@sentry/server-utils`. The `enableTruncation` and `streamGenAiSpans` flags were removed. Gen AI spans are always streamed and never truncated now.

If you imported a helper such as `instrumentOpenAiClient` or `createLangChainCallbackHandler` directly from `@sentry/core`, import it from your platform SDK, such as `@sentry/node`, or from `@sentry/server-utils`.

Remove `enableTruncation` and `streamGenAiSpans` from your config. `addVercelAiProcessors` was also removed. Add `vercelAIIntegration()` instead.
