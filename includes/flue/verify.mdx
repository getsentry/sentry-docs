## Verify

Send a message to one of your agents, then open [Agent Tracing](/product/agents/) in Sentry and select the run. The timeline shows the agent turn, the model calls, the tool executions, token usage, and latency, plus any errors your tools threw.

If no data appears, confirm that:

- The `dsn` belongs to the Sentry project you're viewing.
- `tracesSampleRate` is greater than `0`.
- Your agent completed at least one turn after you added the setup.
