### Link Conversations

Sentry groups a multi-turn chat into a single [Conversation](/product/agents/conversations/) automatically — there's no Sentry-specific setup. Flue already tracks a conversation id per conversation, and the SDK maps it to `gen_ai.conversation.id` on the agent, model, and tool spans of every turn.

The agent span also carries `gen_ai.agent.name`, so you can tell a lead agent's runs from its subagents'.
