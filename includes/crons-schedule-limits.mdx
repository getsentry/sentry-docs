<Expandable title="What's the shortest schedule a monitor supports?">

One minute. Monitor schedules support:

- **Crontab:** Five-field expressions, such as `* * * * *` to run every minute. Six- and seven-field expressions, which add seconds or years, aren't supported.
- **Interval:** Units of `minute`, `hour`, `day`, `week`, `month`, or `year`. There's no `second` unit.

Sentry can't create or update a monitor for a job that runs more than once a minute, such as every 10 or 30 seconds.
