To keep ad blockers from dropping Sentry events, send them through a tunnel on your own origin. Set `tunnel: true` in `Sentry.init`, and your server forwards the events to Sentry:

```javascript {3}
Sentry.init({
  dsn: "___PUBLIC_DSN___",
  tunnel: true,
});
```

Learn how to serve the tunnel on your server, or how to write your own tunnel endpoint, on the <PlatformLink to="/configuration/tunneling/">Tunneling page</PlatformLink>.
